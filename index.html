关于我们 About Us

欢迎来到 Tai Pan

位于英国利物浦市中心的 Tai Pan Chinese Restaurant，自1990年起为顾客提供正宗粤菜佳肴。餐厅秉承"传承经典、追求卓越"的理念，由资深粤菜大师主理。

Welcome to Tai Pan Chinese Restaurant, located in the heart of Liverpool. Since 1990, we have been serving authentic Cantonese cuisine with passion and tradition.
